# OmniSight: Support

OmniSight shows live, open data on a 3D globe on Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. Nothing needs an account or a key to get started: open the app and turn layers on in the Layers panel.

## Getting around

**Apple TV.** Swipe on the remote to move the globe, and press up or down to zoom. Click the centre to open whatever the crosshair has locked on. Play/Pause opens the Layers panel, and Menu closes a panel. If you prefer buttons to swiping, go to **Settings > Remote** and choose **Click buttons**: the arrows then move the globe, and holding the centre or Play/Pause zooms in or out.

**iPhone and iPad.** Drag to move the globe. Pinch or double tap to zoom, and tap a marker to open it. The buttons at the top right open the Layers panel, fly to your location, search for a place and show the legend. On iPad a trackpad, mouse or keyboard also works: scroll to zoom, and use the arrow keys and + and -.

## API keys

A few layers and basemaps use services that need your own free or paid key. Without one they show "Needs API key", and everything else works as normal. Enter keys under **Settings > API keys** at the bottom of the Layers panel. Keys stay in your device's Keychain and are sent only to the service they belong to.

## Common problems

**A layer is empty.** Some layers only cover part of the world, such as US weather alerts and river gauges, or only show when you're zoomed in close enough. Live feeds can also be briefly unavailable. The app keeps what it already has and tries again.

**The map says a tile service is refusing requests.** The service's limit has been reached or its key isn't valid. The app keeps showing the tiles it already has and tries again later. If you entered your own key, check it in **Settings > API keys**.

**"Go to my location" says location is off.** Allow location for OmniSight in your device's Settings, under Privacy. OmniSight uses your location only to fly the globe there when you ask.

**Something looks wrong or out of date.** Note what you see and when, and the app version from **Settings > About**, and email us.
